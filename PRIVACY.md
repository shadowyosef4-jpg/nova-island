# Nova Island privacy policy

*Last updated: November 2, 2026*

Nova Island is a free, open-source app by AmerTech. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Nothing you do in Nova is collected, sold or shared.

## What stays on your PC
Everything Nova shows is read and kept on your PC only:
- the media playing, its title and controls
- downloads, volume, brightness and battery
- clipboard history and files on the shelf
- your settings, reminders and error log

Nova never sends any of this anywhere.

## What Nova reads from the internet
Nova only *downloads* the following. It doesn't send personal data with these requests, beyond what any internet request carries, such as your IP address.

| What | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your approximate location (city level) | ipwho.is, geojs.io or ipapi.co | Prayer times and weather. You can type your city instead in Settings |
| City search | open-meteo.com | Only when you search for your city in Settings |
| Weather | open-meteo.com | The weather in the island |
| Connection check | msftconnecttest.com (Microsoft) | Knowing whether you're online |
| Updates | github.com | New versions (the Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store instead) |
| News from Nova | github.com | Occasional news from the Nova team. Can be turned off in Settings |
| Plugins list and plugins | github.com | Only when you open Settings > Plugins or install a plugin |
| Radio streams | The radio's own servers | Only while you listen with the Quran Radio plugin |

## Microphone and camera
Nova shows a small dot when *another* app uses your microphone or camera. It never records or accesses the microphone or camera itself.

## Children
Nova doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
Changes to this policy are published in this file, in the app's public repository on GitHub.
